SEO basics: helping the right people find your business

Ideas and practical steps to help your business build a stronger digital presence.

SEO · 6 MIN READ
BE FOUND.\nBE USEFUL.

Search engine optimization (SEO) is the process of improving your website so search engines can understand its pages and people searching for relevant services have a better chance of finding it. SEO is not a one-time switch, and no one can honestly guarantee a specific ranking.

Start with what your customers search for

Think about the words a potential customer would use when looking for your product or service. Include natural, descriptive language in page titles, headings and content. Avoid stuffing the same keyword into every sentence—write for people first.

Make pages useful and specific

Give each important service its own page when it deserves a fuller explanation. Describe what you do, who it is for, what the process looks like and how someone can contact you. Helpful, original information is more valuable than generic text written only to repeat keywords.

Set up the technical basics

  • Use a descriptive title and meta description for each page.
  • Keep URLs readable and page headings organized.
  • Make the site mobile-friendly and reasonably fast.
  • Use internal links so pages connect naturally.
  • Set up Google Search Console and submit a sitemap where appropriate.

Be patient and measure progress

Search engines need time to discover, crawl and evaluate pages. Search Console can help you monitor indexing, queries, clicks and technical issues. Use that information to improve pages instead of chasing quick fixes.

SEO is a steady process: create useful pages, make them accessible to search engines, and keep improving based on what visitors need.

Good SEO makes your website easier to understand for both people and search engines. Build the foundations first and keep publishing genuinely useful information.
